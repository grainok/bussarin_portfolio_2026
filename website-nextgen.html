NEXTGEN COMMERCE · THAILAND · GLOBAL COLLABORATION

Website NextGen

Connecting local user needs with a shared global commerce experience.

UX/UI designGlobal & local teamsDesktop & mobile

01 / CONTEXT

A global platform. A local experience.

NextGen Commerce brought together shopping, promotions, subscriptions, registration and post-purchase journeys, with Thailand as the pilot market. I collaborated with the global team and Thailand stakeholders on UX/UI design, reusable components, research activities, usability testing and UAT.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Start with people and their tasks.

Research activities included workshops, internal sessions and in-depth interviews, followed by usability testing of purchase, A Point, order and account journeys. Further testing focused on SOP Manual in the cart. Staff and Amway Business Owners (ABOs) helped the team examine the experience in a local context.

User research

Explore user needs, existing journeys and local expectations through workshops and interviews.

Define the problem

Review friction around promotions, coupon visibility, delivery fees, cart controls and payment or order states.

03 / USER FLOWS

Make complex decisions visible.

Simplified journey summaries for this portfolio, reconstructed from the project scope.

Purchase

Discover → Product details → Cart → Delivery or pickup → Payment → Confirmation → Tracking

Subscription

SOP discovery → Programme details → Product selection → Cart → Subscription setup → Confirmation → Management

Registration

Join → Member or ABO path → Required details → Review → Account

The design needed to account for promotion eligibility, first-time subscription consent, payment failure, registration validation and post-purchase status.

04 / DESIGN & PROTOTYPE

Build clarity into screens and components.

Work with the global team to translate local requirements into screen structure, interaction states and reusable components. Use prototypes and reviews to connect global consistency with Thai content and commerce rules.

The selected designs show Homepage, Product Category, Promotion, SOP, About Amway and Registration / ABO / Member views, with product cards, campaign tiles and mobile navigation.

05 / TEST & ITERATE

Bring staff and ABO feedback into the design.

Participate in usability testing of key journeys with staff and ABOs, review task completion and areas of confusion, and feed findings into design reviews with global and local stakeholders. Refine screens, interaction states and language, then support handoff and UAT.

Usability testing

Understand whether people can follow and complete the intended journey.

UAT & design QA

Check implemented behavior against agreed requirements and review the details before release.

06 / CONTRIBUTION

From research to delivery.

My contribution connected local UX/UI design, component collaboration, Thai content, research and testing activities, and implementation validation within a cross-functional team.

This case study documents scope and design contributions. Quantitative performance results are not included because verified measurements were not supplied.
